    Burning Sandals (AKA Glen Walton) is a DJ and music selector who spins a unique blend of tropical funk, R&B, retro weirdos, jazz, calypso, high life, disco, afro beat, 80s oddities, and more. His sets are known for their eclectic mix of genres and eras, as well as a playful and colourful aesthetic. Let Burning Sandals take you on a journey to the weird and wonderful side of music.
